Getting the app

Not on the App Store yet. TestFlight, today.

Short version: the Mac side is finished and deployed, and the iPhone app is in App Store review. Until that clears you can have the same build through TestFlight — it is the binary that was submitted, not a different one.

TestFlight

Join the beta. You will need Apple's TestFlight app; the link installs Rowel into it. Places are capped while this is new, so it may fill — if it does, the two other routes below still work.

The app is only half of it. Pair it with your own Mac, which needs one command: 

curl -fsSL https://rowel.novabox.ai/install | sh
bridle pair

That prints a QR code. Point the app at it and they are paired — no account, no server address, nothing to sign up for.

Building it yourself

You need a Mac with Xcode, xcodegen (brew install xcodegen), and an Apple ID. A paid account is not required.

  1. Get the source

    git clone https://github.com/0x5446/rowel.git
cd rowel

  2. Generate the Xcode project

    Your Team ID comes from developer.apple.com/account — a free Apple ID has one. 

    ROWEL_TEAM_ID=<your team id> xcodegen generate --spec ios/project.yml

  3. Build to your phone

    Open ios/Rowel.xcodeproj, plug the phone in, pick it as the destination, and run. The first launch needs Settings → General → VPN & Device Management on the phone to trust your developer certificate.

The seven-day catch

An app signed with a free Apple ID stops launching after seven days. Rebuilding resets the clock and keeps your data — it is an annoyance, not a loss. A paid membership makes it a year.

One thing a self-built copy cannot do: lock-screen push notifications. The push entitlement is not available to free accounts, and the relay's push key only covers the released app. Everything else works identically.
